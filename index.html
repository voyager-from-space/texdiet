Why does my LaTeX project take so long to compile?

Drop your project's source zip. texdiet reads every file and ranks what is slowing the compile: giant images, uncompressed PDF figures, TikZ redrawn every time, shell-escape packages and more. It runs in this tab. Nothing is uploaded.

    What changed

      Images are downscaled to at most 2000 px on the long side (about 6.7 in at 300 dpi). Edits in .tex files are marked with % texdiet: comments. Nothing is deleted. Measured compile times for a test thesis are on the timing notes page.

      Sizes and pixel counts are measured from your zip. The impact score is an estimate for ranking, not a measured compile time.

      One route for a paper
